Life Outside Customer Success

A look at how I spend my time when I'm not onboarding customers — the things that keep me sharp, curious, and recharged.

Life Experiences

Life experiences shape our beliefs, perceptions and actions, consciously or unconsciously. Mine is no different.

One of my biggest challenges was my introverted nature. Although awkward and difficult at first, I overcame it over time, especially the social awkwardness. This has helped me in how I interact with people.

I lived in Cyprus between 2013 and 2017 during my undergraduate days. In those years, I met and interacted with individuals from over 50 nationalities, learning, unlearning and relearning a lot. I've also visited Doha, Dubai and Turkey. These experiences opened my eyes to how different cultures are from what I had thought.

I've had setbacks that forced me to grow, and I'm grateful for them, because only through those setbacks was I able to grow and build mental fortitude.

Manga & Anime

The anime I'd rank as my favorites, with my take on each.
Favorite Anime
Vinland Saga
Makoto Yukimura · Wit Studio / MAPPA
A Viking-age epic set in early 11th-century Europe. It follows Thorfinn from a revenge-driven boy to a man searching for a life beyond violence.
My take: It unpacked a lot about Scandinavian culture and the Vikings, but the most intriguing part for me is the character development of Thorfinn.
One Piece
Eiichiro Oda · Running since 1997
Monkey D. Luffy and his crew sail in search of the legendary One Piece treasure. It is the best-selling manga series of all time, and Netflix's anime remake, The One Piece, arrives in February 2027.
My take: By far the greatest fictional story ever written, in my opinion. You have to watch or read it to relate.
One Punch Man
ONE / Yusuke Murata · Madhouse / J.C.Staff
Saitama is a hero who ends every fight with a single punch, and the series parodies superhero and shonen tropes. It began as a webcomic by ONE.
My take: The parody behind the anime is amazing, and it is more enjoyable if you get the humor behind it.
Bleach
Tite Kubo · Studio Pierrot
Ichigo Kurosaki becomes a Soul Reaper and defends the living from Hollows. Thousand-Year Blood War brought the Quincy war to the screen in 2022.
My take: "Bankai" gives me goosebumps every time I hear that word. The clash between Soul Reapers, Quincys and humans is an interesting storyline for me.
Jujutsu Kaisen
Gege Akutami · MAPPA
A dark fantasy about Yuji Itadori and the sorcerers who fight cursed spirits. The manga ended in 2024.
My take: My favorite character is Gojo, and his laid-back personality is really impressive. Fair warning: don't fall in love with a character in JJK. Don't say I didn't tell you.
