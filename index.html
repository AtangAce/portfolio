About
I'm Atang Agwom, a Customer Success Manager who treats customer success as more than ARR, NRR, renewals, and expansion; though those outcomes matter, and I deliver them. To me, it's the bond that ties a customer to a brand through every interaction, check-in, and unscripted moment along the way.
I lead with empathy and back it with strategic, critical thinking. Every conversation is a lever toward a larger goal; every relationship I build is meant to earn loyalty, not just satisfaction; every customer I retain should be willing to vouch for the product. My approach starts with understanding the emotion behind a decision, then applying structure and follow-through to turn that understanding into trust people can rely on.
I help customers build a coherent experience across the full lifecycle — from post-sale onboarding through renewal and expansion — and translate that experience into recurring revenue for the business. The result is a relationship where customers get real value and stakeholders get the return they're looking for.
3+ years in SaaS and managed WordPress hosting, owning onboarding, adoption, renewals and expansion for 80 to 90+ commercial accounts. Based in Nigeria (WAT), available for UK/EU and US East overlap.

The Full CSM Discipline

✓

Customer Health & Risk Detection

I watch for the signals before they show up as churn — drop-off in engagement, quiet accounts, support tickets that hint at frustration. Catching it early is what makes an account recoverable instead of lost.
✓

Adoption & Value Realization

Customers who reach value fast stick around without being talked into it. At MyLangCoach, I built the onboarding milestones and touchpoints that got there, improving active user engagement by 25%.
✓

Renewals, Retention & Expansion

Renewal is earned from day one, not negotiated in the final thirty days. I work with Sales and Product to turn satisfied customers into renewals, and renewals into expansion, holding 95% gross retention across 80+ monthly maintenance renewals.
✓

Stakeholder Management

I build relationships across the account, not just with one point of contact. At Fadama Farmers Microfinance Bank, that approach managed 250+ accounts and grew client referrals by 10%.
✓

Voice of the Customer & Feedback Loops

Customer feedback is only useful if it goes somewhere. I turn what customers tell me into input the product and business can actually act on, closing the loop instead of letting it stall.
