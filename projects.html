Website Rebuild Projects

Four client websites I rebuilt — the goal, the problem, what was expected, what got in the way, and what the client said.

Housekeeping Cleaning

Website rebuild · housekeepingsw.com.au ↗
Website Rebuild Online Booking Conversion
Client Goal
Help people understand the business in detail, the kind of services it provides, and give them an online booking system.
The Problem
The business lacked a strong online presence and had no online booking system.
Expectations
Revamp the website to include more information about the services, create a flow for conversion, and build an online booking system.
Challenge
No major challenges on this project.
Client Feedback
The client was satisfied with the project, especially the booking system.
— Oskar Paino-Povey

Wait a While

Website rebuild · waitawhile.com.au ↗
Website Rebuild Gallery Sound
Client Goal
Give users a virtual preview of the entire tour and let them capture memorable moments. The sound on arrival is important to create a wildlife feeling.
The Problem
The website didn't have enough information or moments from the tours.
Expectations
Revamp the website to have a natural feel and include memories from past events.
Challenge
Deciding which moments to include in the gallery — it took a while to settle on the best ones.
Client Feedback
The client was happy with the website and the information included on it.
— Paul

George Halls Auto Service

Website rebuild · georgehallsautoservice.com.au ↗
Website Rebuild Online Booking Conversion
Client Goal
Revamp the website and set up an online booking system.
The Problem
The online booking system was not working and traffic was not converting.
Expectations
Set up a working online booking system and revamp the website to gain more conversions.
Challenge
No major challenges on this project.
Client Feedback
The client was satisfied with the website redesign and the successful online booking system.
— Steven Hall

One Tech Solutions

Website rebuild · onetechsolutions.com.au ↗
Website Rebuild Logo Redesign Lead Generation
Client Goal
Give users all the information at their fingertips and trigger calls to action so people reach out.
The Problem
The website was outdated and was getting little traffic.
Expectations
Revamp the website to generate more traffic and stronger calls to action.

The main challenge was the conflict around the logo redesign and implementing it as per the client's request.
Client Feedback
The client was satisfied with the rebuild and the logo redesign, which captures the simplicity he wanted. Traffic increased during post-launch QA.
